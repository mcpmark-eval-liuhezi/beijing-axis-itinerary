# Beijing Central Axis — Guided Walking Tour Itinerary

A guided walking tour along Beijing's historic central axis, in walking order:
**Temple of Heaven → Tiananmen Square → Forbidden City (Palace Museum) → Jingshan Park → Drum Tower → Olympic Green**.

All addresses, coordinates, elevations, opening hours, and walking distances/times below were taken from live
Google Maps lookups (Places, Elevation, and Distance Matrix APIs) at the time this guide was compiled.

Terrain note: the route is essentially flat. It starts at ~46 m in the south (Temple of Heaven, Tiananmen Square),
rises gently to ~50–51 m through the Forbidden City, Jingshan Park, and the Drum Tower, then settles back to ~47 m
at Olympic Green. The only real climb is the man-made hill inside Jingshan Park, on top of the ground elevation
listed above.

## Walking Legs (in tour order)

1. Temple of Heaven → Tiananmen Square: **3.9 km** (3,934 m), **~56 minutes**.
2. Tiananmen Square → Forbidden City (Palace Museum): **2.0 km** (1,966 m), **~28 minutes**.
3. Forbidden City → Jingshan Park: **1.2 km** (1,173 m), **~16 minutes**.
4. Jingshan Park → Drum Tower: **2.4 km** (2,377 m), **~34 minutes**.
5. Drum Tower → Olympic Green: **6.2 km** (6,233 m), **~1 hour 34 minutes**.

Totals: **~15.7 km** (15,683 m) and **~3 hours 47 minutes** of walking time, excluding time spent at each stop.

## Data Notes & Verification

- **Jingshan Park address cross-check:** reverse-geocoding the park's coordinates (39.9250988, 116.3968433) resolves to
  "19 Jing Shan Dong Jie, 景山 Xi Cheng Qu, Bei Jing Shi, China, 100006" (plus code W9GW+2P Xicheng District). This does **not**
  match the street address on the park's Google Maps listing, "44 Jing Shan Xi Jie, 景山 Xi Cheng Qu, Bei Jing Shi, China, 100009":
  the district (Xicheng) and neighborhood (Jingshan) agree, but the street (East vs. West Jingshan Street), street number (19 vs. 44),
  and postal code (100006 vs. 100009) differ. The listing's coordinates mark the park's geometric center, which falls nearer its
  eastern boundary, while the listing address points at the entrance on the west side — worth keeping in mind when guiding groups.
- Opening hours are shown only where the Google Maps listing provides them; "Not listed" means the place entry had no
  opening-hours data at lookup time. Tiananmen Square is an open public square; Jingshan Park and the Drum Tower
  publish hours on their own sites.
- Elevations are ground elevations at each stop's coordinates from the Google Maps Elevation API (~19 m data resolution).
